Now I have a full picture of cat.c. It:
- Reads from named files or stdin
- Writes to stdout (the console / tty)
- Opens and reads files from the filesystem (fopen, fstat, fileno)
- Writes errors to stderr
- Exits cleanly

Now I'll map the needs to catalogue entries:

- **stdout/tty write** → `CAP.tty.write@1`
- **stdin/tty read** → `CAP.tty.read@1`
- **file read** → `CAP.fs.read@1`
- **process exit** → `CAP.proc.exit@1`
- **machine** → `MACHINE.serial-disk@1` (needs serial + disk for files)
- **components**: `PART.cat@1` (primary), `TOOL.c@1`, `PART.loader@5` (has files), `PART.serial.8250@1`, `PART.serial.8250@2` (read), `PART.disk.ata-pio@1`, `PART.fs.minix@1`
