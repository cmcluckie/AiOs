2. **`__udivdi3`.**  The counter's numbers are 64 bits; a 32-bit processor
   divides them with a helper the compiler expects from its own library
   (libgcc), which nothing in AiOs links.  Linux divides them with do_div
   (arch/x86/include/asm/div64.h), and the hand lift keeps it (tsccal.c line 77).
   The three files divided with a plain `/`.

3. **Every one of those words reached the suite's output and none reached the
   builder.**  jig/test prints a failed build's log, then the line `FAIL ...
   build of stopwatch failed` (out/agent/race-timer/jobs/race-timer-1-try1/
   suite.txt has both).  The agent's judge kept the FAIL line and dropped the
   rest.  The lifter's prompt already says no C library, every warning an error,
   the port instructions in inline assembly; what it never got was which rule a
   hand-in broke, and where.

4. **The fix (decision 0111).**  A failed lift's answer now carries what jig/test
   printed just before its FAIL line: the last 15 lines, at most 1,500
   characters, each program's path cut to its name.  TST.agent.lift has a lift
   that does not build, and checks the builder is shown the compiler's line (red
   before the fix, with the answer the race's builders got; a seventh mutation).

5. **Read by Claude: not fixed by this.**  The two files that built measured
   wrong; how often that happens shows only once hand-ins get past the build.
   The builder's fix path (judge::verdict) shows the same suite lines only, and
