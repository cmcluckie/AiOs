# Findings 0042: Dave's sieve lifted, and the drag flavor builds

Source: part 24, 2026-11-08, after petition 0015 (decision 0119).  Built in
Claude's workspace from a copy of the repo, under emulation; not on the jig.
Goes to: the architects (Chris and Claude).

1. **What the builder said was missing**, with petition 0015 signed:
   "PART.drag@1  part not in the catalogue" and "PART.libc.musl@2  part not in
   the catalogue" (out/drag/missing.txt).  Both lifted here, by copying, no
   model:
   - lifted/PART.drag@1: PrimeC/solution_1's sieve.c, kept as drag.c, and
     prime-check.h, whole and untouched, from jig/primes.pin.
   - lifted/PART.libc.musl@2: PART.libc.musl@1's 178 files and 38 more from
     musl 1.2.4 at jig/musl.pin: the 29 source files jig/libc pieces says the
     sieve takes beyond cat's (84 pieces in all, as on the jig, findings 0039),
     and the 8 headers they and the sieve need that @1 did not have; each
     checked byte for byte against the musl tree, every blob in its origin
     record.

2. **Three things the builder needed changing.**
   - It built musl only at @1; it now builds the version a program's catalogue
     entry depends on (jig/aios, libc_musl).
   - A program's flags could only be -std=, -D or -include=.  Dave's entry is
     built with -Ofast -march=native -mtune=native -funroll-all-loops, and a
     drag race entry is its author's build, so -O, -march=, -mtune= and -f with
     no value are taken too (SPEC.builder.3@2; TST.builder.flags@1, a fifth
     mutation; -fplugin=evil.so is still refused).
   - Rule 11 refused the flavor: its kernel exposes CAP.tty.read@1, because
     PART.loader@10 depends on PART.serial.8250@2, which has a receive side.
     The sieve never reads the console.  Petition 0015 now asks for it, with
     SPEC.serial.2@1 as the reason, so the flavor builds: a debt, written down
     here.  The kernel that leaves the receive side out when no petition asks
     for it is for later.

3. **The drag flavor builds**: "built flavor drag: PART.loader@10 (574b6334ec133d89)
   + PART.drag@1 (ELF, 54288 bytes)".  Booted on its machine under emulation,
   Dave's sieve stops at once: "Guru Meditation #00000006.080493A5", an
   instruction the processor would not run (QEMU's emulated processor has no
   AVX at all; on the jig it would be AVX that AiOs has not switched on).  The
   three red tests are red for their own reasons now, not for a flavor that
   does not build: "the call was refused: clock_gettime64 -38"; "mmap2 failed
   at round 235 mmap2 -12"; "the program was stopped: Guru Meditation
   #00000006.0804907E".  Ready for the 3090's builders.
