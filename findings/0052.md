# Findings 0052: call a friend

Source: part 24, 2026-11-27, the 3090's second run on plan-dragshare at 0ea701d
(rung 1, out/agent/plan-dragshare/), and Chris, by voice, the same day.
Goes to: the architects (Chris and Claude).

1. **Rung 1 failed too.**  Three tries, 100, 55 and 57 minutes, each to its 60
   calls.  Try 1 built and booted; its Linux program stopped at address 0
   ("Guru Meditation #0000000E.00000000 program 2 stopped").  Tries 2 and 3 did
   not build.  The plan stopped stuck.

2. **What the card could not see.**  Every space on the sharing kernel is mapped
   at the same window, PROGRAM_BASE (win_lo and win_hi are one pair for the
   whole gate).  A Linux program file is linked at 0x08048000.  Try 1 copied the
   file's pieces into the space as offsets from the first piece, read the entry
   point as an offset into the file (entry_off, which it then checked against
   the file's length), and built the stack partly at window addresses and partly
   at the file's.  Nothing in the test's output points at the window: it says a
   program stopped.  Claude offered two ways out: give each space its own window,
   or link the Linux program at AiOs's address.  Chris chose neither.

3. **Chris's way: the card calls a friend.**  The card does the work; when it is
   out of its depth it calls Claude, who sends a briefing, not a fix: what is
   actually wrong, where in the repo to look, a few lines of example.  The card
   still writes the code.  "We should never let the 3090 go 60 rounds without
   calling a friend."  For this first test, Claude only briefs.

4. **When a builder calls** (decided with Chris by voice): at call 20 with
   nothing handed in, or after two hand-ins that did not build, whichever comes
   first, and never later than call 30; once a builder.  The card's wins took 7,
   10, 16 and 27 calls (findings 0045, 0047); at 10 it would have called on
   half the jobs it won alone.  Car 3's builders, which spent forty calls
   reading and handed in code that did not build, would have called every time.

5. **When a director calls:** once, the first time the part is stuck, with the
   plan and the part's log; the briefing comes back to it as the answer to its
   last job, the stuck count starts again, and the part goes on.  Stuck a second
   time, it stops for strategic as before.

6. **What it costs, and where it shows.**  Claude Sonnet 5.5 on Bedrock, priced
   in jig/prices.toml.  The plan sets the friend's share ([friend] max_usd);
   every call is asked of what is left of it and of the month's cap first, with
