# Findings 0051: car 3 in one job was too big; a ladder

Source: part 24, 2026-11-25 to 26, the 3090's first run on plan-dragshare at
41978d1 (out/agent/plan-dragshare/).  The suite ran on the jig; the new tests'
red was seen in Claude's workspace, under software emulation, not on the jig.
Goes to: the architects (Chris and Claude).

1. **What came out.**  Three tries, 39, 70 and 78 minutes, each to its 60
   calls; 1, 2 and 4 hand-ins.  None built: every hand-in failed
   TST.gate.drag-shares@1 with "build of dragshare failed", the compiler's own
   words shown under it (try 3: "invalid use of undefined type 'struct
   gate_elf'").  The plan stopped stuck: the same two reds three runs in a
   row, the second TST.loader.quiet-exit@1, red before it began.

2. **Why.**  The job asked for all of it at once in one red test: tell a Linux
   program file from the bytes, place its pieces, lay out its starting stack,
   start it, and give every space its own brk, mmap edge and holes.  Try 1
   spent most of its calls reading (share.c, elf_run, gate_space_new, the test,
   jig/test, jig/aios) and its last ones on the per-space rename; from call 49
   on it was dropping old answers to make room.  The test says only "build
   failed" or "no result line" until everything is right.  Chris bet an hour;
   Claude bet over three.  Neither: not at all, in this shape.

3. **A ladder, one rung a test, as the ladder of petitions goes.**
   - Rung 1, TST.gate.elf-shares@1 (SPEC.gate.18@1): a Linux program file that
     prints "elf hello" and exits, packed after the stopwatch.  Red at this
     commit in the workspace: "Guru Meditation #0000000E.00040000 program 2
     stopped", the gate starting the file at its first byte.
   - Rung 2, TST.gate.elf-heap-shares@1 (SPEC.gate.19@1): the same, taking and
     giving back memory in the sieve's order 20,000 times.
   - Rung 3, TST.gate.drag-shares@1: Dave's sieve.
   Both probes are tests/programs/dragcalls.c (modes HELLO and ORDER), packed
   with `aios pack`, run by tests/programs/dragshare-rungs.sh; run on Linux in
   the workspace they print their lines and exit 0.

4. **One rung a plan, signed between.**  Wins still do not stack inside a plan
   (findings 0044 item 2), so the plan has rung 1 alone; its pass is signed and
   the next rung starts from that commit.
