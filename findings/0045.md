# Findings 0045: AVX and the munmap hole, the 3090's second run

Source: part 24, 2026-11-10 to 11, the 3090's second run on plan-drag at
9ad84bb (agent/plans/drag.toml).  The suite ran on the jig; read from
out/agent/plan-drag/.
Goes to: the architects (Chris and Claude).

1. **What came out.**  Nine tries in the plan's 480 minutes.  TST.gate.avx@1
   green twice (try 3, 46 calls; try 6, 49 calls), TST.gate.munmap-keep@1 green
   three times (tries 4, 7 and 8; try 4 in 7 calls).  Tries 1, 2 and 5 ran out
   their 60 calls and broke the cat flavor's build, as in the first run.

2. **The sieve ran.**  On both AVX passes TST.drag.passes@1 went green with
   them: 28,100 passes in 5 s (try 3) and 28,763 (try 6), on the jig, under KVM.
   Car 1, the jig's Linux, was 24,797 to 25,097 (findings 0039).  Practice laps:
   each from a builder's copy, not a race; findings 0046 is the race.

3. **The wins did not stack, again** (findings 0044 item 2): AVX and the
   munmap fix were never in one copy.  Taken together here by hand: try 6's AVX
   and try 4's munmap, laid on 9ad84bb as the 3090 wrote them.  Every flavor
   still builds in Claude's workspace, but "both", refused by rule 19 as before.

4. **Why try 6 and not try 3.**  Try 3 saves each program's registers with
   XSAVE into a 576-byte area; with x87, SSE and AVX on, XSAVE writes 832, so
   every save runs 256 bytes past the area into whatever follows it.  It also
   never loads the mask XSAVE reads from EDX:EAX.  With one program nothing
   follows that matters, so its tests were green.  Try 6 turns AVX on at boot
   (CR4.OSXSAVE, XCR0 = 7) and keeps FXSAVE, which writes its 512 bytes and no
   more.

5. **What try 6 still lacks, for car 3.**
   - FXSAVE does not keep the top halves of the AVX registers, so two programs
     using AVX would trample each other's.  No test sees it yet: one program
     never shares the registers.
   - Its XSETBV passes EDX as clobbered, not as 0; XCR0's top half is whatever
     EDX held.  It held 0 on the jig.
   Both are the 3090's, with car 3's tests.

6. **Red before and after: TST.loader.quiet-exit@1.**  "interrupts on on the
   gate's way out, so a tick can land there: 3 of 1167414 times through".
   Red on 9ad84bb before any builder touched it; not the 3090's doing.  Not yet
   looked at.
