# Findings 0048: the race, car 1 against car 2

Source: part 24, 2026-11-25, out/race24.sh on the jig at 9a029bd.  The jig:
Linux 6.18.55, AMD Ryzen 9 5900X, 4 cpus, itself a Hyper-V machine.  Read from
what the script printed, pasted by Chris.
Goes to: the architects (Chris and Claude).

1. **One file, both cars.**  out/drag/drag.bin as the drag flavor builds it
   (sha256 c92b33e76f49a742..., a static 32-bit Linux program): Dave Plummer's
   PrimeC/solution_1 with his own flags, musl 1.2.4, sieving to 1,000,000 for
   5 seconds and checking every pass itself ("faithful=yes").  Car 1 runs it on
   the jig's Linux.  Car 2 boots it alone on AiOs (flavor drag, PART.gate@2,
   MACHINE.serial-timer@1) under KVM, a machine inside the jig.  Heats back to
   back, car 1 then car 2, three each.

2. **Passes in 5 seconds:**

   | heat | car 1, the jig's Linux | car 2, AiOs alone |
   |------|------------------------|-------------------|
   | 1    | 24,990                 | 29,036            |
   | 2    | 24,527                 | 29,186            |
   | 3    | 25,028                 | 28,621            |

   Car 1 averages 24,848, car 2 28,948: car 2 about 16% ahead.  Car 2's slowest
   heat beats car 1's fastest.  Car 1 in earlier runs that day, the same file:
   22,987 to 25,032 (findings 0046).

3. **The tests after it, on the jig:** TST.drag.passes@1 (29,263 passes),
   TST.gate.avx@1, TST.gate.clock@1, TST.gate.munmap@1,
   TST.gate.munmap-keep@1 and TST.gate.munmap-order@1: 6 passed, 0 failed.

4. **What is not the same between the cars.**
   - The clock.  Linux answers the sieve's clock() with the processor time the
     program used; AiOs with milliseconds since boot (findings 0044).  With one
     program they should agree; TST.gate.clock@1 holds AiOs's to the host's
     clock within 1% (7 ms in 10 s here).
   - Car 2 runs one level deeper: a machine inside the jig, which is a machine
     inside Windows.
   - Memory.  Linux maps fresh pages on every mmap2 and takes them away on every
     munmap, about 3,000 times a second (findings 0039); AiOs hands out room it
     already has, zeroed.  Not measured: the likeliest reason car 2 wins.
   - Nothing else runs on AiOs.  The jig's Linux has its own work.

5. **Not raced yet: car 3**, AiOs with several programs.  PART.gate@2 does not
   keep the top halves of the AVX registers across a switch (findings 0045 item
   5).
