the card (qwen3.8:27b, 98,304 tokens) with the jig at a9c25ac and the plans at

5. **Read by Claude: what the race found, beyond the score.**
   - A builder that cannot see why a build failed spends its calls guessing.
     On the timer that is most of every run, on both benches.  The test, not
     the model, is the first thing to change: say what the compiler said.
   - The stuck rule compares which tests failed, not how close a run came.
     On a part with two tests it called run 1 stuck after its closest try.
   - A director that sees only notes will invent what happened.  On the timer
     one hint said a builder "never once compiled" after ten hand-ins, and
     told the next to build its own file, which a builder cannot.  Another
     hint, the one that told the builder how to set the 8254 up, moved the
     next hand-ins from measuring nothing to measuring.
   - The director gave a hint on the first job of serial run 3 from the
     model's own knowledge, the ready-bit mistake both benches made; nothing
     carried over from earlier runs (each run's log starts empty).  An
     advantage jig/sit has no seat for.
   - A failing layered run is slow: it uses every call it has.  A passing one
     is fast.
   None of these was changed during the race, so the runs are alike.
