# Findings 0057: what the friend costs, and Bedrock's cache

Source: part 24, 2026-11-28, the 3090's run on plan-dragshare at c04a268
(rung 2), job plan-dragshare-1, friend-1.md and friend-2.md there; the first run
with friend@3.  The change below was run in Claude's workspace on saved replies.
Goes to: the architects (Chris and Claude).

1. **The friend as built in findings 0055, on its first job.**  Called at call
   20 (nothing handed in): 10 looks, 110,447 tokens in, 4,599 out, $0.4003.
   Called again at call 22 (hand-in 1 still not a pass): 9 looks, 288,624 in,
   12,470 out, $1.0529.  $1.45 for one builder.  Every place in both briefings
   is a file, a line and lines quoted from it; neither has any working out in
   it; the second begins "The first briefing's fix changed nothing", and says
   why.

2. **Both were wrong about the cause.**  The first: the window is mapped to the
   file's length, not the region's (it is not: both are the region's 8 MiB, as
   the second found in share.c).  The second: the 4 MiB entries of space_map
   are marked 0x87, "U/S bit (bit 2) clear, kernel-only"; 0x87 has bit 2 set.
   It read the right lines and drew the wrong conclusion.  Three hand-ins
   after them, the test said what it had said before: Guru Meditation
   #0000000E.08738000, and from QEMU's record, "fault 0e at 00100dc8 touching
   08738000": the kernel itself, clearing what mmap2 was about to hand out.

3. **Claude's reading, not tested:** space_map maps the middle of a window with
   4 MiB entries, which the processor takes only at 4 MiB boundaries.  AiOs's
   own programs' window starts on one; a Linux program's, at 0x08048000, does
   not, so the middle entry's address has low bits set that the processor
   reads as something else.  The friend is Sonnet 4.6, the one Claude this
   account can call (findings 0053 item 1); a stronger one is the account's to
   turn on.

4. **Why it cost what it did.**  A call is a conversation: each look sends
   again everything before it.  Ten looks at up to 12,000 characters, on top of
   the changed file whole, is 110,000 tokens at the first call and 288,000 at
   the second, all at the full price.

5. **The change.**  The friend's turns are marked for Bedrock's cache
   (Converse's cachePoint, after the system prompt and after the last message),
   so a turn pays the cache-read price, a tenth, for all the turns before it
   and the cache-write price, a quarter more, for what is new.  The tokens
   read and written are costed at jig/prices.toml's own prices for them and
   recorded in runs/ (RUNS_CACHE_READ, RUNS_CACHE_WRITE, which jig/runs has
   taken since the exam).  A look answers 8,000 characters at most, not 12,000.
   By the prices, a call like the second would cost about forty cents.  Not yet
   seen from this account; AGENT_FRIEND_CACHE=0 turns it off if Bedrock
   refuses the mark.  TST.agent.friend@1, two more mutations, each red.

6. **Not exact:** before each turn the friend's share is asked for what the
   uncached tokens so far cost plus $0.50; the cache's tokens are counted only
   when the call ends.  The share can be passed by a few cents.
