4. **The card, measured 2026-10-27.**  Ollama 0.40.8 in WSL.  qwen3.8:27b at
   65,536 tokens of context spilled 6% onto the processor (18 GB, the card at
   23,147 of 24,576 MiB); at 49,152 it sits 100% on the card (17 GB).  Runs use
   MODEL_NUM_CTX=49152.  Sonnet's five taken tries ended with conversations of
   about 19,000 to 31,000 tokens (their input.txt, a byte count over four, a
   rough measure); its long failed tries on the system calls reached about
   80,000, more than the card can hold.  It is a thinking model: its thinking is
   counted in its output tokens and not kept, and a reply cut off at the
   16,000-token cap is now said to be (decision 0086).
