3. **What it got right: what the program needs.**  The console to print to,
   memory, the clock and exit, each a capability AiOs already names, in
   the shape of a petition, from reading the sieve alone.

4. **What it missed: how a Linux program file runs on AiOs, which is nowhere in
   Dave's source.**  It chose PART.loader@4 and MACHINE.serial-only@1, the
   stopwatch's loader on a machine with no timer, so the clock it asked for
   could not run; it left out the C library, the 64-bit divide, the gate that
   runs a program in ring 3 and answers its Linux calls, the timer parts, and
   AVX; and it named reasons, a component and tests that do not exist
   (SPEC.sieve.1 to 3, of which SPEC.sieve.1@1 is BYTE's sieve from part 11;
   PART.sieve.prime@1; TST.sieve.passes-line@1 and count-positive@1).  Cat's
   draft in part 19 missed the same kind of thing.

5. **petitions/0015.toml, corrected by Claude from the draft**, every change:
   - Capabilities kept: tty.write, mem.alloc (reason SPEC.gate.14@1, munmap),
     time.read (SPEC.gate.13@1, the clock call), proc.exit; their reasons moved
     to specs that exist.  Added: cpu.ring3 (SPEC.gate.9@1), as cat's has;
     dev.serial, dev.pit and hv.kvmclock, as the stopwatch's has, for the
     timer; cpu.avx (SPEC.gate.15@1), new, for the 4 AVX instructions.
   - Components: PART.drag@1, Dave's sieve, lifted, in place of the invented
     PART.sieve.prime@1; PART.libc.musl@2, the C library with the pieces the
     sieve takes beyond cat's; PART.divide64@1; PART.loader@10 and PART.gate@2
     in place of loader@4; the timer parts, PART.timer.tsc-pit@2 and
     timer.kvmclock@1; PART.serial.8250@2; MACHINE.serial-timer@1 in place of
     serial-only@1.
   - Tests: TST.drag.passes@1 (new: the sieve alone prints its own result
     line, with at least one pass, and exits 0: car 2) and the three red tests
     from findings 0040.
   The referee reads it: "petition 0015: well-formed, 11 component(s) pending
   admission".  The drag flavor's build refuses it until a decision signs it.
