# Findings 0044: two of three, and why they did not add up

Source: part 24, 2026-11-08 to 09, the 3090's first full run on plan-drag at
99d1af7 (agent/plans/drag.toml; kept in out/agent/plan-drag-run1-keep/).  The
suite ran on the jig; the checks on the new test below ran in Claude's
workspace, under emulation, not on the jig.
Goes to: the architects (Chris and Claude).

1. **What came out.**  Seven tries of ten, about seven hours.  Try 2 made
   TST.gate.clock@1 green in 16 calls, 14 minutes.  Try 3 made TST.gate.munmap@1
   green in 10 calls, 13 minutes.  Tries 1 and 4 to 7 ran out their 60 calls.
   AVX was never green.  The director stopped and asked.

2. **Why the wins did not add up.**  Every fix job starts from the plan's
   commit.  The clock fix from try 2 was not under try 3, nor the munmap fix
   under try 4.  A lifter is handed the last lifter's file (decision 0114); a
   fixer is handed nothing.  The director spent tries 4 to 7 asking builders to
   get two or three green at once, which no builder on a clean commit had been
   asked to do before.  That is the harness, not the 3090.  Left for later:
   carrying a fix plan's pass forward.

3. **The director's question, answered.**  "Is the cat-flavor build break a
   known jig/cache artifact?"  No.  gate.c is in every flavor that shares the
   gate, and warnings are errors.  The 60-call tries left code in it that broke
   cat; the two short tries that passed broke nothing.

4. **The two passes, taken as the 3090 wrote them.**  Both diffs laid on 99d1af7
   together in pool/PART.gate@2/gate.c: clock_gettime64 (403) answers
   CLOCK_PROCESS_CPUTIME_ID from the ms door, and munmap gives room back.  Every
   flavor still builds in the workspace, but "both", which is refused by rule
   19, as before.

5. **A hole in the munmap, the test did not see.**  Memory is handed out
   downward.  The 3090's munmap moves the low edge up to the top of whatever is
   given back.  Give back an older block while a newer one is still in use and
   the newer one is handed out again.  TST.gate.munmap@1 only ever gives back
   the newest.  TST.gate.munmap-keep@1 (SPEC.gate.17@1) is red on it: "a block
   still in use was handed out again".  With the edge moved only when the block
   given back is at it, it is green, as are TST.gate.munmap@1 and
   TST.gate.clock@1; that change is not made: it is the 3090's to find.

6. **AVX.**  Five of the seven runs where the AVX test ran stopped with an
   invalid instruction at the program's AVX instruction (Guru Meditation
   #00000006).  gate.c turns SSE on beside where AVX would be turned on; the
   builders never got there.

7. **Second run.**  The plan is now gate.avx@1 and gate.munmap-keep@1, from the
   commit with the two passes in it.
