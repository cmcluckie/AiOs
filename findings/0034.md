# Findings 0034: the "stopped printing" message was wrong

Source: part 23, the timer rerun of 2026-11-07 at b16b30b (decision 0112), run 1,
stopped by Chris during its third builder.  Every number is from the builders'
logs, out/agent/plan-timer/jobs/plan-timer-1.err to plan-timer-3.err.  Goes to:
the architects (Chris and Claude).

1. **What decision 0112 got wrong.**  tests/programs/msprobe.c prints the door's
   milliseconds only when they have reached the next half second.  A door stuck
   at 0 prints "0" once and nothing after it, so its capture is one line, and
   one line spans no time.  Decision 0112's check called any capture spanning
   less than half the seconds "stopped printing: it died or hung while running",
   so it said that of every door stuck at 0.  TST.timer.says@1 did not catch it:
   its capture of zeros was eleven lines, one a second, which msprobe never
   prints.  Claude wrote the capture without reading msprobe.c.

2. **What the builders were told.**  Of the 24 hand-ins judged before the run
   was stopped, 6 did not build, 16 ran the full time (11 or 12 seconds) and were
   told the machine stopped printing after 0 seconds, and 2 measured a rate:
   plan-timer-2's hand-ins 11 and 12 failed only on "first reading 147 ms" and
   "148 ms, expected 5 or less", the clock counted from before the loader
   started it.  None of the 16 was a crash by the time it took.

3. **The fix (decision 0113).**  The span rule is gone.  An empty capture is
   "the machine printed nothing"; a door stuck at 0 is "the door said 0
   throughout" again.  TST.timer.says@1's capture of zeros is now the one line
   msprobe prints; the test is red with b16b30b's check and with the check before
   decision 0112, and its one mutation is killed.  Checked in the repo on the
   device, by the test's own probe; not on the jig.
