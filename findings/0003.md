# Findings 0003: the terms, vetted against the industry

Source: the glossary added to CLAUDE.md on 2026-10-11, checked word by word
against what the same word already means in systems software.  Goes to: Chris.
Each line says whether the word collides, with what, and what to do.  Nothing
here is changed yet; the decisions are the holder's.

## Collisions that matter

| Term | Already means | Risk | Recommendation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **AiOS** | AIOS, "LLM Agent Operating System", Rutgers, 2023, an active GitHub project and arXiv paper; "AI OS" is also a category name startups use | High.  In print "AiOS" and "AIOS" are the same word.  Search results for the name go to them. | Know it before the series gets far.  Options: keep the name and let "AY-ohss" and the tagline carry the difference; or rename while nothing is attached to it.  The holder's call. |
| **flavor** | Linux distributions call their variants flavours (Ubuntu's Kubuntu, Xubuntu); OpenStack flavors are VM sizes (CPU, RAM); Android product flavors are build variants | Medium.  The distro meaning is close to ours and is the LinkedIn hook ("a new flavor of Linux"), so it may help.  Cloud people will hear "VM size". | Keep if the distro connotation is wanted.  If not, **charter** is free: the set of petitions one machine has been granted, in the same legal register as constitution and petition. |
| **segment** | x86 memory segments, which our loader literally sets up (the GDT); ELF segments, which our loader is loaded from | High, and it is a collision inside our own code. | Rename.  **part** fits the parts-bin metaphor and is plain.  **cutting**, in the horticultural sense (a piece cut from a plant and rooted on its own), fits lift and cut exactly but is twee.  Recommend part. |
| **door** | Solaris Doors, a fast RPC mechanism (1996), still in illumos | Low.  Different enough, obscure enough, and the plain meaning carries. | Keep.  Note Solaris in the glossary. |
| **capability (CAP)** | Capability-based security (seL4, Capsicum, Fuchsia handles); Linux's own CAP_SYS_ADMIN and friends, literally named `CAP_*` | Medium.  Kernel people will assume Linux capabilities, which are coarser and process-wide. | Keep.  The spirit is the same: a thing a holder may touch.  Glossary should say it is not Linux's CAP_* list. |
| **loader** | Bootloaders (GRUB, systemd-boot); the dynamic loader (ld.so) | Medium.  Ours is neither: it is entered by the firmware like a kernel, and runs a program like a kernel.  It is a kernel with one tenant and no sharing. | Keep for now; it is honest about what it does today.  When sharing arrives it becomes a kernel and the word changes with it. |
| **sensor** | IoT hardware; "probe" is the software term (Kubernetes liveness probes); SRE says SLI | Low. | Keep.  Sensor is the word the design doc uses and it reads plainly. |
| **referee** | Nothing in software.  The nearest industry concept is a Kubernetes admission controller or a policy engine (OPA) | None. | Keep.  Say "admission controller" once when talking to Kubernetes people. |
| **admission** | Kubernetes admission control: exactly this, a gate that checks a request before it enters | None; it helps. | Keep. |
| **well-formed** | XML: syntactically correct, as opposed to valid | None; it helps.  Same two-phase idea. | Keep. |
| **scaffold** | Rails scaffolding generates boilerplate; construction scaffolding is temporary support | Low.  The construction meaning is ours. | Keep. |
| **jig** | Manufacturing and test jigs: a fixture that holds work while it is made, not part of the product | None; it is exactly right and unusual in software. | Keep. |
| **pool** | Thread pools, ZFS pools, connection pools: a shared reserve | Low.  Generic. | Keep. |

| **finding** | Audit and security reports | None; same meaning. | Keep. |

## Terms the industry has that we should use where they fit

- **Vendoring** is what the industry calls copying third-party source into
  your tree.  Lifting is vendoring with provenance and surgery.  Say "vendored,
  with the hands cut" once to kernel people and they will understand.
- **Unikernel** is the nearest existing thing to hello world plus the loader:
  one program and the minimum runtime, linked into one image, no sharing.
  Ours differs in that the program and the loader stay separate (the door),
  and in that it grows by petition.  Expect the comparison; have the sentence
  ready.
- **Reproducible build** is the industry phrase for what the `reproducible`
  field records.  Use it.
- **SBOM** (software bill of materials) is what a petition's component list is
  to a security auditor.  A petition is an SBOM with reasons.
