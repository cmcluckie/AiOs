Trust.  Don't write the code, write the spec.  We're telling developers not to look at the code.  So far I have not looked at a single program besides hello world.  The only screens I've looked at are the pass/fails.  You're thinking: Chris, you're crazy, you need to look at the application.  You tell me: who's ever written a unit test for a hello world program, then come back and ask me that.  I purposely haven't looked.  That's the whole point: we have to trust AI with some of this to get the real speed out of these tools.  Should you go as far as I have?  Absolutely not.  Can you trust it a little more with good governance and QA?  Absolutely.  And the argument: real software developers, me included, don't get offended, make far more mistakes than the current AIs do, especially Claude.  Real-world experience.
